# Fold & Fetch

## Register

product

## Users

Hackathon visitors play a gentle 60–90 second foldable-screen platform puzzle. The player holds a device, glances between Kaprao and the route, and needs immediate, readable controls that leave the miniature rooftop visible.

## Product Purpose

Guide Kaprao, a rounded golden and cream corgi with teal scarf and small RGB sunglasses, across a cozy rooftop repair shop. Swipe left or right to move, up to jump, down while airborne to dash. Adjust the physical fold to tune chute depth and height. Release the beam to bridge the gap, then reach the bell.

## Brand Personality

Warm, playful, resourceful. Toy-like smooth forms, restrained night lighting, cream and teal with copper and small cyan or peach neon details.

## Anti-references

Avoid low-poly geometry, aggressive cyberpunk clutter, luminous interface chrome, blur, glass cards, dashboard widgets, or labels baked into illustrations. UI should feel native and quiet.

## Design Principles

Keep the player, fold, chute, and falling beam readable. Teach one action at its moment of need. Give Release a clear target. Keep pause and audio secondary. Celebrate the bell with one clear Replay action.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Readable cream/ink contrast, shape and label cues alongside color, minimum 44pt touch regions when integrated, scalable system type, concise gesture labels, reduced motion. Native safe areas and hinge-reserved regions must drive placement; artwork coordinates are design references, not device guarantees.
