# Palette and material sheet

`palette.json` contains twelve exact sRGB hex colors, their OKLCH equivalents, intended usage, roughness, metallic values, and emission intent. `palette.css` is an optional design token reference. `material-sheet.svg` keeps labels editable; `.png` is the preview.

The UI is restrained: cream and ink do most of the work, deep teal identifies the primary action, and bright cyan or peach belongs to small world lights. Corgi gold is warm and matte. Copper is the only strongly metallic base material. Roughness and metallic values are art-direction starting points, not measured real-world material data.

Use smooth geometry and broad soft highlights. Avoid faceted low-poly silhouettes, mirror-polished plastic, a neon wash across the whole scene, or emissive UI surfaces. Warm lamps reveal cream edges and copper details while ink keeps the scene readable at night.

Approved text pairs: ink/cream 9.77:1; deep teal/cream 6.07:1; muted/cream 5.13:1; cream/night 12.29:1. Cyan, peach, copper, leaf, and gold are not body-text colors on cream.
