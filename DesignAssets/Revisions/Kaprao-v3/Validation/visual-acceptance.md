# Visual acceptance before export

Compare the actual model render with `Concepts/kaprao-authoritative-furred-reference.png`, using front, side and three-quarter views under neutral lighting. A generated reference image does not pass this gate.

- Natural dog face: inset eyes with eyelids, a shaped nose with nostrils, a projecting muzzle and an open smiling mouth with coherent lips and jaw.
- Fur contributes to the silhouette around cheeks, chest and ears; no smooth bald coat or detached teardrop pieces.
- Coat markings follow the reference: honey-orange back/head, cream chest and cheeks, white forehead blaze. No plate-like raised eyebrows.
- Short sturdy legs and natural paws beneath a long, chunky corgi body; distinct limbs for rigging.
- Soft ear thickness and furry interiors; no flat triangular ear plates.
- Teal scarf reads as fabric with folds. Glasses fit the face and are removable without damaging eye geometry.
- The three views depict one consistent character; no good front view hiding a broken side profile.
- Four motions preserve the face, fur and scarf without detached pieces, visible body seams or root-motion drift.

Native compatibility, texture containment and performance checks follow this visual gate. They cannot substitute for it.
